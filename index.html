(FAQ)

Got specific questions?

Contact us
How fast do you deliver?

Logos arrive within one week. For shops, we confirm the launch date before you pay a deposit.

What do I pay, and when?

50% to start and 50% on delivery. Prices are fixed, so there are no surprise invoices.

Do I need a meeting?

Rarely. Fixed packages run on a short form and email. Larger projects get one short kickoff call.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo and brand colours if you have them. For a store, a product list and photos. No photos yet? We can generate product images for you.

What if I want changes?

One round of revisions is included. We build to the design you approved, so changes are usually small.

Is there anything you do not do?

We keep it to two things: websites and branding. We do not run ads or manage social media.

Still have questions? Talk to us on a free call.
